FAQ

Questions, answered.

A few things students usually want to know before joining the chapter.

Who can join ISTE?

Students interested in technology, projects, learning and leadership can join the chapter.

Do I need prior technical experience?

No. Activities are designed for students with different levels of experience.

What kind of events does ISTE conduct?

Workshops, technical sessions, project activities, competitions and industry-focused sessions.
